# Acceptable Use Policy

Do not use Acme Cloud for:
- Illegal activity
- Spam or phishing
- Reverse engineering the service
- Unauthorized access attempts
- Storing malware
- Cryptocurrency mining
- Excessive automated scraping outside the documented API rate limits
- Impersonating Acme Cloud or other people
- Reselling the service itself without a partnership agreement

## Enforcement

- First violations usually receive a warning with a fix-by date.
- Serious or repeated violations result in account suspension or termination.
- You can appeal enforcement actions by emailing `support@acme.example`.

Violations may result in account suspension.
