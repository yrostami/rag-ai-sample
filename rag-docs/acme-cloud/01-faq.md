# Frequently Asked Questions

## What is Acme Cloud?
Acme Cloud is a team collaboration platform for projects, files, and comments.

## How do I reset my API key?
Go to **Settings > API Keys**, click **Regenerate**, and copy the new key. The old key stops working immediately.

## How many users can I invite on the Free plan?
The Free plan supports up to 5 users.

## Does Acme Cloud support SSO?
SSO is available on the Pro and Enterprise plans.

## Is data encrypted?
Yes. Data is encrypted at rest with AES-256 and in transit with TLS 1.3.

## How do I export my data?
Go to **Settings > Export**, choose CSV or JSON, and select a date range.

## How much storage do I get?
Free 2 GB, Pro 100 GB, Enterprise custom. Maximum file size is 25 MB on Free, 1 GB on Pro, and 5 GB on Enterprise.

## Can I use two-factor authentication?
Yes. Enable TOTP-based 2FA under **Settings > Security > Two-Factor Authentication**. It is available on all plans.

## Where is my data stored?
Choose `us-east` or `eu-west` when creating a workspace. Data residency is pinned at creation and cannot be changed later.

## Are webhooks available on the Free plan?
Yes. Free allows 3 webhook endpoints, Pro 10, Enterprise custom.

## What roles exist?
Owner, Admin, Member, and Viewer. Viewers are read-only. See Roles and Permissions for the full matrix.

## What happens when I invite someone?
They receive an email invite that expires after 14 days. Resend it from **Settings > Members** if it expired.

## Can I cancel my subscription?
Yes. Downgrading takes effect at the end of the billing period and workspace data is kept.

## Do you offer an SLA?
Enterprise has a 99.9% uptime SLA with service credits. Pro has a 99.5% target. Free has no SLA.

## Can deleted data be recovered?
Deleted files stay in the project trash for 30 days. Full workspace restores from daily backups go through support.

## Is there a status page?
Yes, at `status.acme.example`, with an RSS feed for incident updates.

## How do I report a security issue?
Email `security@acme.example`. Do not disclose publicly before a fix is available.
