# Privacy Policy

We collect account data, usage logs, and billing information.
We do not sell personal data.
We retain usage logs for 90 days.
Users can request deletion by emailing `privacy@acme.example`.
Data is processed in the EU and US.

## What we collect

- Account data: name, email, workspace membership.
- Usage logs: feature events and API request metadata, kept 90 days.
- Billing information: processed by our payment processor; we never store full card numbers.

## Legal bases and your rights (GDPR)

- We process account data to provide the service, usage logs for security and product improvement.
- You can request access, correction, or erasure of your personal data by emailing `privacy@acme.example`.
- Enterprise customers can sign a Data Processing Agreement (DPA) via `privacy@acme.example`.

## Subprocessors

- Cloud hosting provider (primary storage and compute)
- Email delivery provider (transactional email)
- Payment processor (billing)

## International transfers

Data transfers outside the EU rely on standard contractual clauses.

## Cookies

We use a session cookie for login and optional analytics cookies that you can decline.

## Children

Acme Cloud is not intended for users under 16.
